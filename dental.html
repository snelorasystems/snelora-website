Questions

Questions clinics ask us

Will patients know Sam is AI?

Yes. Sam tells every caller it is your clinic's AI assistant and that calls may be recorded.

Does Sam replace my receptionist?

No. Sam handles routine calls like bookings and common questions, and sends urgent calls to your staff while you're open. Your team stays in charge of patient care.

What if Sam can't help a caller?

Sam takes a message and emails it to your team, marked urgent for emergencies. While you're open, it can transfer urgent calls to your staff.

How does Sam handle dental emergencies?

Sam gives a safety message first: call 911 or go to the ER for spreading swelling, fever, or trouble breathing or swallowing, and seek emergency care for a knocked out tooth, uncontrolled bleeding or a facial injury. Then it takes an urgent message or offers your earliest emergency exam slot.

Is an AI receptionist safe for a dental clinic?

Sam never gives dental or medical advice, never diagnoses and never suggests medication. It is open about being AI and points emergencies to 911 or the ER.

Can Sam confirm whether we accept a patient's insurance?

Sam answers insurance questions in general terms. It doesn't confirm whether a specific provider is accepted; it takes a message for your team instead.

How does booking work?

Sam books, reschedules and cancels appointments in your calendar using the caller's phone number. The patient gets a text confirmation and a reminder.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days with unlimited minutes and no contract. Sam answers all your incoming calls 24/7 during the trial. We set it up with your hours, services and calendar, and you decide at the end whether to continue.

Extra minutes are $0.45 each. We message you at 80% of your minutes, and your line is never switched off.
