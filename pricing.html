Questions

Common questions

How does the free pilot work?

You get 14 days and up to 150 minutes to try the receptionist on real calls, with no contract. We set it up with your hours, services and calendar, and you decide at the end whether to continue.

Which plan should I choose?

It depends on how many calls your receptionist will handle. The pilot shows your real call minutes, so we can recommend a plan based on your own numbers rather than a guess.

What happens if I go over my minutes?

Your line keeps working. Extra minutes are $0.45 each before HST, and we message you when you reach 80% of your plan so there are no surprises.

Do unused minutes roll over?

No. Minutes reset at the start of each month on both monthly and annual plans.

Are text messages extra?

Text messages for confirmations and reminders are included within normal use.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The receptionist tells callers it is an AI assistant, and it transfers urgent calls to a person on your team.
