One pattern, every workflow

Works by your rules. Hands off what matters.

Owners tell us the same thing: they want AI to take the routine work, not run the business. So every agent we build follows the same loop. It handles what your rules cover, and anything outside them comes to you.

1

Something comes in

A phone call, a support ticket, a review, a low-stock alert.

2

AI does the work

It answers, books, sorts or drafts, following your hours, prices and rules, and flags anything unusual.

3

You stay in charge

Routine work runs on its own. Emergencies, sensitive replies and purchases come to you first. You decide where that line sits.

4

It gets done

The booking lands, the ticket is routed, the reply goes out, and every step is logged.

Why it holds up

Built by a QA engineer.

Most AI demos work on the happy path. A decade in quality engineering means we start from the question: what happens when it goes wrong?

Guardrails first

Risky items get escalated, not guessed.

A one-star review still gets a draft, but a careful one — no admitting fault, no promises — and it can't go out without the owner's sign-off.

Tested to break

Bugs get found here, not by your customers.

Every build is tested scenario by scenario, including the edge cases: lost edits, bad data, invalid links, a service being down.

Your data, locked

Secure by default.

Logins with hashed passwords, API keys kept out of code, and an audit log of every action, so you always know what happened and who did it.
